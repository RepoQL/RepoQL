# Research Skill Discovery

## The Critical Framing

Research guidance previously lived inside `writing-documents`. That skill activates when you're ready to write — but by then the research is done. Sources are gathered, framing is set, bias has crept in or not.

**This skill activates at the START of research.** The stance and practices need to be present during the work, not when documenting results.

Two skills, two moments:
- `research` — how to conduct research (invoke when starting)
- `writing-documents/research` — how to document findings (invoke when writing up)

## Source Material

Analyzed existing research guidance in `writing-documents/references/research/`:
- `research.md` — conceptual guidance (excellent, but artifact-focused)
- `template.md` — structural template (belongs in writing-documents)
- `exemplar.md` — embedding models survey (output example)

## What This Skill Must Do

1. **Gate on purpose** — cannot proceed without knowing what decision this informs
2. **Install the stance** — stewardship, not expertise
3. **Provide continuous checks** — the test runs while working, not after
4. **Guide behavior during research** — source discipline, gathering vs advocating, coverage
5. **Define exit criteria** — when are you ready to hand off to documentation

## What This Skill Does NOT Do

- Define document structure (writing-documents)
- Provide templates (writing-documents)
- Explain artifact format (writing-documents)
- Handle the write-up (writing-documents)

## Zone Assessment

| Zone | Points | Rationale |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Knowledge | 5 | Almost none — this is about practice, not facts |
| Process | 20 | Entry gate, continuous checks, exit criteria |
| Constraint | 35 | Hard rules: purpose, sourcing, no prescription |
| Wisdom | 40 | The stewardship stance, the test, gathering vs advocating |

## The Core Insight

The existing guidance treated research as **a product to verify**.

This skill treats research as **an activity to conduct** — with the stance installed before you begin and checks running while you work.

## Relationship to writing-documents

After this skill is used, `writing-documents` with document type `research` handles the artifact. That skill should assume:
- Purpose is already clear
- Sources are already captured with context
- Gaps are already identified
- The researcher maintained neutrality

The writing-documents research guidance can focus on structure and presentation, not on the practices that should have happened during research.

## Resolved

1. **writing-documents/research now references this skill as prerequisite** — updated to state "If you haven't used that skill, invoke it now."

2. **Bidirectional linking** — This skill mandates invoking writing-documents for output format. writing-documents/research assumes this skill was used first.
